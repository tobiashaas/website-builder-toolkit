# Starting points

Four style positions to begin variants from. Each is a position on the axes in `SKILL.md`, a list of what it rules out, and the way it usually fails. They are craft judgment, not research (see `docs/EVIDENCE.md`, Gaps).

## Prohibitions

- Do not hand over a starting point as the result. Move at least one axis for this brand and say why.
- Do not take colors, fonts or imagery from a starting point. They come from the brand profile.
- Do not pick a starting point by its name or by what is trending. Pick it by the page's job and the material the brand really has (photos, products, text).
- Do not show two variants from the same starting point. Variants differ on at least two axes.

## Type-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | airy |
| Type contrast | strong |
| Shape | sharp |
| Color budget | one-accent |
| Imagery | type-only |

- **Fits:** advice, law, writing, software with no photogenic product. Brands whose proof is what they say.
- **Rules out:** hero images, icon rows, cards with shadows, more than two type sizes above the fold.
- **Fails as:** a large headline that says nothing. Check: the headline holds a fact or a claim from the brand profile, and a competitor could not paste it.

## Work-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | regular |
| Type contrast | medium |
| Shape | soft |
| Color budget | one-accent |
| Imagery | photo |

- **Fits:** trades, studios, local services, anyone with real pictures of finished work.
- **Rules out:** stock photos, illustrations of people, photo filters that change the material's color, text over the busy part of an image.
- **Fails as:** a gallery without order. Check: every photo shows the brand's own work and has a caption that names it; the first viewport holds one photo, not a slider.

## Range-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | dense |
| Type contrast | flat |
| Shape | sharp |
| Color budget | one-accent |
| Imagery | photo |

- **Fits:** shops, product ranges, directories, reference pages. Readers who compare and scan.
- **Rules out:** full-height hero sections, centered text blocks, decoration between rows, hover-only information.
- **Fails as:** clutter. Check: one row height and one gap per list, numbers right-aligned, body text at 16 px or more on phones, filters reachable by keyboard.

## Story-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | airy |
| Type contrast | strong |
| Shape | sharp |
| Color budget | two-families |
| Imagery | illustration |

- **Fits:** case studies, reports, campaigns, about pages. One reader path from top to bottom.
- **Rules out:** card grids, sidebars, more than one action per section, illustration styles from different sets.
- **Fails as:** a long scroll with no reason to continue. Check: every section answers the question the section before it raised; the page works with the illustrations removed.
