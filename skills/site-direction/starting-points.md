# Starting points

Five style positions to begin variants from. Each is a position on the axes in `SKILL.md`, the material it needs, a list of what it rules out, and the way it usually fails. They are craft judgment, not research (see `docs/EVIDENCE.md`, Gaps).

A starting point is chosen by the material the brand really has, never by its industry. Two joiners can need different ones: one has photos of finished work, the other has a price list and nothing to show.

## Prohibitions

- Do not use a starting point when the brand profile sets a style position under `style`. The profile's position stands; see "Variants" in `SKILL.md`.
- Do not hand over a starting point as the result. Move at least one axis for this brand and say why.
- Do not take colors, fonts or imagery from a starting point. They come from the brand profile.
- Do not pick a starting point by its name, by the brand's industry or by what is trending. Pick it by the "Needs" line: if the brand cannot supply that material, the starting point is out.
- Do not show two variants from the same starting point. Variants differ on at least two axes.
- Do not set `colorful` from a starting point. None uses it; choose it only when the brand profile lists three or more color families with a job each.

## Type-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | airy |
| Type contrast | strong |
| Shape | sharp |
| Color budget | one-accent |
| Imagery | type-only |

- **Needs:** a claim or a fact strong enough to stand alone, from the brand profile. No images required.
- **Rules out:** hero images, icon rows, cards with shadows, more than two type sizes above the fold.
- **Fails as:** a large headline that says nothing. Check: the headline holds a fact or a claim from the brand profile, and a competitor could not paste it.

## Work-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | regular |
| Type contrast | medium |
| Shape | soft |
| Color budget | one-accent |
| Imagery | photo |

- **Needs:** real photos of the brand's finished work or product, enough for every section that shows one.
- **Rules out:** stock photos, illustrations of people, photo filters that change the material's color, text over the busy part of an image.
- **Fails as:** a gallery without order. Check: every photo shows the brand's own work and has a caption that names it; the first viewport holds one photo, not a slider.

## Range-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | dense |
| Type contrast | flat |
| Shape | sharp |
| Color budget | one-accent |
| Imagery | photo |

- **Needs:** many comparable items with the same fields (products, services with prices, entries, dates), and readers who compare and scan.
- **Rules out:** full-height hero sections, centered text blocks, decoration between rows, hover-only information.
- **Fails as:** clutter. Check: one row height and one gap per list, numbers right-aligned, body text at 16 px or more on phones, filters reachable by keyboard.

## Story-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | airy |
| Type contrast | strong |
| Shape | soft |
| Color budget | two-families |
| Imagery | illustration |

- **Needs:** one reader path from top to bottom (a case, a report, a campaign, a history) and illustrations from one hand, or the budget to commission them.
- **Rules out:** card grids, sidebars, more than one action per section, illustration styles from different sets.
- **Fails as:** a long scroll with no reason to continue. Check: every section answers the question the section before it raised; the page works with the illustrations removed.

## People-led

| Axis | Stage |
| --- | --- |
| Density | regular |
| Type contrast | medium |
| Shape | round |
| Color budget | two-families |
| Imagery | photo |

- **Needs:** real portraits of the people readers will deal with, with names and roles they agreed to publish.
- **Rules out:** stock portraits, group photos as the only image, initials or icons in place of faces, a team grid before the page says what the people do.
- **Fails as:** a friendly page without an offer. Check: the first viewport says what the reader can get and from whom; every portrait has a name, a role and a way to reach that person.
